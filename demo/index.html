Media mix simulator

Split a monthly ad budget across three channels and see the range of outcomes a long sales cycle could produce. Each result is a band: the low end, the middle and the high end of 2,000 simulated runs.

Illustrative assumptions. The click prices, conversion rates and deal value on this page are invented planning ranges, listed at the bottom. They describe no real account. Replace them with your own before you rely on any number here.

    What this model leaves out

    It treats channels as independent and the funnel as the same for every channel. It has no limit on how much a channel can absorb, no learning period, no seasonality and no delay between a lead and a deal. The deal counts in the table are averages and can be fractions; the two chance figures use whole deals. A result is only as good as the ranges that went in.
